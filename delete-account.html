Delete your account

KD Hand Cricket · Updated September 25, 2026

You can permanently delete your KD Hand Cricket account and the personal data linked to it at any time. You can do this inside the app, or by email if you no longer have the app.

Option 1: Delete in the app (fastest)

  1. Open KD Hand Cricket and sign in.
  2. Tap Profile in the bottom menu.
  3. Scroll down and tap Delete Account.
  4. Read the summary, type DELETE in the box, then tap Delete to confirm.

Your account is deleted immediately and you are signed out. If you are in a match or a tournament, finish or leave it first.

Option 2: Request deletion by email

  1. Send an email to kdgamings1114@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. If you signed in with Google, send it from that Google account's email address, so we can confirm the account is yours.
  3. Include your in-game display name or username if you know it.

We confirm by email and complete the deletion within 30 days. We may ask you to confirm ownership before deleting anything.

Guest accounts: a guest account is not linked to any email or name, so we cannot verify who owns it by email. Please use Option 1 on the device you play on. Uninstalling the app also removes your guest ID from that device.

What is deleted

  • Your profile: name, email address, Google account ID, profile picture, phone number, username, and guest ID
  • Your coin and diamond balances, and your full coin and diamond transaction history
  • Your owned and equipped cosmetics
  • Your player statistics, points, and leaderboard ranking
  • Your daily reward, login streak, and mission progress
  • The sign-in session and data stored by the app on your device (when deleting in the app)

Deletion is permanent and cannot be undone. Unused coins and diamonds are lost and are not refunded.

Signing in again later with the same Google account or on the same device creates a brand-new, empty account.

Data held by Google

Google Play keeps its own record of purchases made through Google Play, and Google Mobile Ads handles advertising data under Google's Privacy Policy. You can manage these in your Google Account and your device's ad settings.

Deleting only some of your data

We do not currently offer deletion of individual parts of your data while you keep your account. To remove your personal data, delete your account using one of the options above. For any other privacy request, email kdgamings1114@gmail.com.
